About daysbetweendays

A free date calculator, with no sign-up.

daysbetweendays tells you how many days there are between two dates. Pick a start date and an end date and you get the answer straight away, along with the same gap in weeks, and in years, months and days.

People use it to count down to a holiday or event, work out how long ago something happened, check a notice period or deadline, or track a milestone such as a project length or a streak.

How the calculation works

  • The count runs from the start date up to, but not including, the end date. For example, 1 January to 2 January is 1 day. If you want to count both the first and last day, add 1 to the result.
  • Leap years and the different lengths of months are handled automatically.
  • If the end date is earlier than the start date, the two are swapped, so you always get a positive number.
  • The "years, months and days" breakdown counts whole calendar months first, then the remaining days.
  • The calculation happens in your browser. The dates you pick are not sent to our server or stored.

What it doesn't do

The calculator counts calendar days using the Gregorian calendar. It does not exclude weekends or public holidays, and it does not take time zones or times of day into account. If you are working out a legal, contractual or financial deadline, please check the result against the exact rules that apply to you. The results are provided for general information and are not legal or financial advice.
